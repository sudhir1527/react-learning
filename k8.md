## 2. The Comprehensive Swing Trading Curriculum

### Phase 0: Setup and Realistic Expectations [NEW] (Week 1)
* **Psychological Baseline:** Understanding the learning curve; treating trading as a business with operational costs rather than a lottery.
* **Capital Segregation:** Separating trading capital strictly from emergency funds, daily living expenses, or long-term financial goals.
* **Infrastructure Setup:** Opening a demat and trading account with an Indian broker. Comparing discount vs. full-service brokerages.
* **Tooling Infrastructure:** Configuring a charting platform (e.g., TradingView), a rule-based screener (e.g., Chartink, Screener.in), and bookmarking the NSE/BSE official corporate announcement portals.
* **Journal Establishment:** Setting up a digital trade and observation log (Spreadsheet or dedicated journal tool) on day one.

**Milestone:** All software tools are operational, the journal spreadsheet is active, and a strict learning plan with defined maximum risk caps has been documented.

---

### Phase 1: Market Foundations and Indian Market Mechanics [CORE, EXPANDED] (Weeks 2-4)
* **Exchange Structure:** NSE, BSE, Nifty 50, Nifty 500, and sectoral indices. Role of clearing corporations and depositories (CDSL/NSDL).
* **Order Book Dynamics:** Bid-ask spreads, liquidity pools, market depth, slippage, and volume analytics.
* **Order Types:** Market, Limit, Stop-Loss Market (SL-M), Stop-Loss Limit (SL-L), and After Market Orders (AMO). Understanding execution trade-offs.
* **Product Types:** Cash Delivery (CNC) vs. Intraday Margin (MIS) vs. Futures & Options (F&O). *Swing trading primarily utilizes Cash Delivery.*
* **Indian Settlement Rules:** T+1 settlement cycles. Mechanics and structural risks of Buy Today Sell Tomorrow (BTST), specifically short-delivery auction risks.
* **Circuit Limits:** Understanding upper and lower price bands (5%, 10%, 20%) and the structural risk of getting trapped in illiquid, lower-circuit stocks.
* **Surveillance Frameworks:** Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM), and Trade-to-Trade (T2T) segments. Rules for avoiding these structural traps.
* **Short-Selling Constraints:** Understanding that overnight short positions are legally prohibited in the Indian cash equity segment. Short swing trading requires F&O derivatives **[DEFER]**.
* **F&O Ban List:** How a stock entering the F&O ban impacts liquidity and volatility, even in the underlying cash market.
* **Transaction Friction & Costs:** Calculating the mathematical impact of Brokerage, Securities Transaction Tax (STT), Exchange Transaction Charges, SEBI Turnover Fees, GST, Stamp Duty, and DP (Depository Participant) charges on sell orders.
* **Taxation Realities:** Understanding Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) tax laws (currently 20% plus applicable surcharge and cess for assets held $\le$ 12 months with STT paid). Differentiating capital gains from business income based on trading frequency.

**Milestone:** Calculate the exact round-trip friction costs of a hypothetical 50,000 INR trade and pass a written self-test on circuit limits and auction risks.

### Phase 2: Chart Reading and Market Structure [CORE] (Months 2-3)
* **Price Representation:** Candlestick anatomy (body, wicks, open, high, low, close). De-emphasizing complex pattern names in favor of pure buying/selling pressure analysis.
* **Trend Identification:** Dow Theory principles. Defining uptrends (higher highs, higher lows), downtrends (lower highs, lower lows), and sideways ranges.
* **Key Levels:** Horizontal Support and Resistance zones, role-reversal concepts (broken resistance becoming support), and swing structural pivots.
* **Dynamic Structures:** Trendlines, channels, ascending/descending triangles, rectangles, and flag structures.
* **Multi-Timeframe Analysis:** Analyzing the Weekly chart for structural macro context and the Daily chart for execution triggers.
* **Stage Analysis:** Applying Stan Weinstein’s 4-Stage framework (Stage 1: Accumulation, Stage 2: Advancing, Stage 3: Distribution, Stage 4: Declining).

**Milestone:** Successfully mark structural zones and current Weinstein stages on 50 historical charts without using a single technical indicator.

### Phase 3: Risk Management Foundations [CORE, MOVED] (Month 4)
* **The Core Formula:** Position Sizing = (Maximum Account Risk in INR) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price).
* **Account Risk Model:** Restricting total risk per trade to a fixed percentage of total capital (e.g., 0.5% to 1% maximum).
* **Technical Stop Placement:** Placing protective stops based strictly on price structure (e.g., below a swing low or a consolidation base) or volatility metrics (Average True Range - ATR), never based on arbitrary monetary values.
* **R-Multiple Concept:** Expressing all trade outcomes as a multiple of initial risk ($R$). Aiming for structural asymmetric setups where potential reward outpaces risk ($> 2R$).
* **Slippage & Gap Risks:** Preparing for black swan gap-downs where execution fills occur significantly lower than the specified stop-loss level.

**Milestone:** Build a functional position-sizing calculator spreadsheet. Complete 20 hypothetical setups where size automatically scales based on stop-loss distance.

### Phase 4: Market Regime and Macro Context [CORE] (Month 5)
* **Index Alignment:** Determining the broader market health using the Nifty 50 and Nifty 500 trends. Trading with the wind at your back.
* **Market Breadth:** Monitoring internal indicators such as the percentage of stocks trading above their 50-day and 200-day Moving Averages.
* **Sectoral Rotation:** Identifying leading sectors vs. lagging sectors using NSE sectoral indices (e.g., Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Auto).
* **Volatility Index:** Reading the India VIX to gauge institutional fear and option pricing expectations.
* **Institutional Activity:** Analyzing Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) and Domestic Institutional Investor (DII) net daily cash flows as contextual background data.
* **Macro Events:** Tracking the corporate earnings calendar, RBI Monetary Policy announcements, and the Union Budget to manage event risk.

**Milestone:** Develop a weekly market regime checklist that classifies the market into one of three states: Aggressive Long, Defensive/Reduced Size, or Cash/Stand Aside.

---

### Phase 5: Technical Indicators & Selection [CORE / OPTIONAL / DEFER] (Month 6)
* **Moving Averages [CORE]:** Utilizing the 10-EMA, 21-EMA, 50-SMA, and 200-SMA for trend tracking, dynamic support/resistance, and mean reversion filters.
* **Relative Strength (RS) [CORE]:** Calculating Mansfield Relative Strength or price ratio charts to find stocks outperforming the Nifty 500 index. *Note: This is structural relative strength, not the standard Relative Strength Index (RSI).*
* **Volume Analytics [CORE]:** Analyzing Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP) and identifying institutional accumulation days (high volume up-days).
* **Momentum Oscillators [OPTIONAL]:** Using the Relative Strength Index (RSI) purely for identifying momentum shifts and extreme divergences.
* **Advanced Visualizations [DEFER]:** Postponing complex concepts like Volume Profile, Keltner Channels, Stochastics, Rate of Change (ROC), and intricate Fibonacci extensions until simple price-and-volume strategies show edge.

**Milestone:** Build automated screeners on Chartink or TradingView that isolate liquid stocks trading above their 50-SMA while showing positive Relative Strength against the Nifty 500.

### Phase 6: Systematic Swing Setups [CORE] (Months 7-8)

#### Setup A: The VCP / Volatility Contraction Pattern Breakout
* **Logic:** Identifying institutional accumulation where price ranges narrow from left to right as supply is systematically absorbed.
* **Triggers:** Entry on a daily closing breakout above the pivot line accompanied by above-average volume.
* **Stop Placement:** Positioned immediately below the low of the final contraction cheat area.

#### Setup B: The Orderly Pullback / Moving Average Touch
* **Logic:** Buying strong Stage 2 uptrending stocks experiencing an orderly, low-volume profit-taking pullback.
* **Triggers:** Reversal candlesticks (e.g., hammer, bullish engulfing) off the rising 21-EMA or 50-SMA.
* **Stop Placement:** Located below the recent swing low formed by the pullback sequence.
